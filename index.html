Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions

What types of electronics do you accept?

+
We accept all types of electronics including laptops, desktops, monitors, printers, servers, networking equipment, phones, tablets, keyboards, mice, cables, and more. If you're unsure about an item, just contact us!

Is the collection service really free?

+
Yes! Our primary model is FREE collection for donated e-waste. We also offer a paid collection service for those who prefer compensation for their equipment. The choice is yours!

How secure is your data destruction process?

+
We use certified degaussing and physical destruction methods that meet industry standards. Our highly qualified team follows strict protocols, and we provide a Certificate of Destruction for your records. We can perform destruction on-site at your location or in-house at our secure facility.

What happens to my old electronics?

+
Functional devices are refurbished and sold at affordable prices to schools and underserved communities. Non-functional items are professionally dismantled, and materials are recovered for recycling. We have a zero-landfill commitment - nothing goes to waste!

Do you serve my area?

+
We serve clients throughout Kenya and are expanding nationwide. Contact us to confirm service availability in your specific location. We offer both pickup services and drop-off options.

Can I purchase refurbished equipment?

+
Absolutely! We offer tested and certified refurbished computers, peripherals, and accessories at budget-friendly prices. Contact us to learn about current inventory and special pricing for schools and nonprofits.
